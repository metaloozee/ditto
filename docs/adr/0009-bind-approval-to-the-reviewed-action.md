# Bind approval to the reviewed action

The Desk and the relevant conversation show the same approval request. The owner authorizes one execution through its action-specific control. This identifies which request the owner meant when several actions are waiting.

Approval covers the version shown to the owner. For an email, that includes the sending account, recipients, subject, body and attachments. Changing any of those details requires fresh approval. A one-time decision leaves the bot’s standing permissions intact.

Refusal or expiry blocks that action. Preserve finished work, continue independent work within scope and tell the owner what remains blocked. The bot cannot keep asking for the same action or carry it out through another channel.
