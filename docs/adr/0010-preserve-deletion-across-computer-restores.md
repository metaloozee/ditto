# Preserve specialist deletion across computer restores

Confirming deletion stops the specialist, rejects its queued work and immediately revokes access to its conversation, memory, screenshot cards and browser profile. Storage cleanup can retry. If every available backup would expose deleted data, keep the computer stopped and explain why recovery is unavailable.

Ordinary screenshot cards expire seven days after capture and leave the step description in the conversation while the specialist exists. A file explicitly published as a deliverable, including an image, belongs to the workspace owner and remains accessible until the owner deletes it.
